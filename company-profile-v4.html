OV Company Overview

Google LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., is the world's leading technology company with dominant positions in search, online advertising, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. The company's search engine processes over 8.5 billion queries per day, commanding approximately 91.6% of the global search engine market share.

In 2025, Google significantly accelerated its AI strategy with the expansion of Gemini, its multimodal large language model, across all consumer products and enterprise services. Google Cloud Platform (GCP) achieved a $40 billion annualized revenue run rate, growing at 28% year-over-year, positioning it as the third-largest cloud infrastructure provider globally behind AWS and Microsoft Azure.

The company continues to invest heavily in hardware through its Pixel smartphone line, Tensor chip development, and quantum computing research via its Willow quantum processor. Google's advertising business, spanning Search, YouTube, and its Display Network, remains the primary revenue driver, generating over $237 billion in advertising revenue in the trailing twelve months.
