About Google

Google LLC is a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. and the world's most widely used search engine, processing over 8.5 billion searches daily. Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin at Stanford University, Google has evolved into a diversified technology conglomerate with products spanning cloud computing (Google Cloud Platform), mobile operating systems (Android), video streaming (YouTube), hardware (Pixel, Nest), and artificial intelligence (DeepMind, Gemini).

Alphabet's advertising business remains its primary revenue driver, contributing approximately 77% of total revenue. Google Cloud Platform is the company's fastest-growing segment, achieving $43B in annual revenue with 29% YoY growth in FY2025.

Frequently asked questions — Google

What does Google do?
Google is a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. that operates the world's largest search engine, Google Cloud Platform, Android mobile OS, YouTube, and a digital advertising network generating the majority of Alphabet's $350B+ annual revenue.
Who are Google's main competitors?
In search: Microsoft Bing, OpenAI ChatGPT. In cloud: AWS, Microsoft Azure. In advertising: Meta Platforms, Amazon Ads. In AI: OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta AI.
How many employees does Google have?
As of Q1 2026, Alphabet employs approximately 182,502 full-time employees globally.
What are Google's recent acquisitions?
Google acquired Wiz, Inc. for $32 billion in March 2026. Other recent acquisitions include Cameyo (2025) and Photomath (2024).
What is Google's annual revenue?
Alphabet reported $350.02 billion in revenue for FY2025, up 14% YoY. Google Cloud contributed $43B, growing 29% YoY.
