Section 01

Executive Summary

June 2026 — A month defined by AI consolidation, energy transition mega-deals, and Southeast Asian platform wars.

June 2026 at a Glance

June 2026 recorded 11,200+ competitive intelligence events across 45 markets — a 19% increase versus May 2026, driven primarily by an acceleration in AI-related M&A, sovereign energy commitments, and a flurry of strategic alliances in the enterprise software space.

The global AI race intensified with Microsoft's $4.2B acquisition of Inflection AI's enterprise division, Google's Gemini Ultra 2.0 launch, and SAP's deep partnership with NVIDIA — collectively signalling that enterprise AI is moving from pilot to production at scale. The AI sector alone accounted for 23% of all tracked events and 38% of all capital deployed in funding rounds this month.

In energy, the QatarEnergy-TotalEnergies $95B LNG supply agreement stands as the single largest deal tracked by Contify in its 8-year history, reshaping European energy security calculus through 2053. Meanwhile, ADNOC's completed acquisition of Covestro signals Gulf sovereign wealth is firmly in the downstream chemicals business.

Southeast Asia's platform economy saw its most transformative week in two years, with GoTo's TikTok Shop merger creating Indonesia's largest social commerce entity. Grab's $1.2B raise from Temasek and GIC underscored continued institutional confidence in the ASEAN super-app model despite global tech de-rating trends.

Section 02

Key Findings

Six headline findings from 11,200+ events analysed across 45 markets in June 2026.

Finding 01
AI Consolidation Accelerates
48 AI-related M&A deals in June — a new monthly record. Hyperscalers are acquiring AI startups at a rate 3x higher than 12 months ago, compressing the independent AI company lifecycle.
Finding 02
Energy Mega-Deals Dominate
The QatarEnergy-TotalEnergies deal ($95B) and Saudi Aramco's $50B LNG investment mark the largest energy commitments since the Paris Agreement era — defying green transition pressure.
Finding 03
Southeast Asia Platform Wars Intensify
GoTo-TikTok merger and Grab's $1.2B raise signal a new phase of consolidation. The ASEAN e-commerce and fintech battle is now a 3-way contest: Grab, Sea Limited, and the new GoTo-TikTok entity.
Finding 04
Gulf Sovereign Wealth Goes Downstream
ADNOC's Covestro acquisition and Saudi PIF's battery manufacturer investments signal a strategic shift: Gulf states are now competing to own post-crude value chains rather than just monetising hydrocarbons.
Finding 05
India's Dual Engine Firing
Reliance Jio (₹25,000 Cr raise) and Indian IT giants (TCS $2.1B deal, Infosys AI launch) demonstrate India's dual-engine growth story: domestic digital infrastructure + global IT services exports.
Finding 06
Sovereign AI Sovereignty Emerges
Three major sovereign cloud partnerships — e&/Microsoft (MENA), Singtel/Google (ASEAN), DBS/proprietary — suggest AI sovereignty is the next geopolitical battleground after energy sovereignty.
