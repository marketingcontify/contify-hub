Intel Announces 14,000-Person Layoff — 15% Workforce Reduction in Restructuring

Intel confirmed a 15% global workforce reduction affecting approximately 14,000 employees across engineering, marketing, and administrative functions. CEO Pat Gelsinger cited the need to "reduce cost structure and reshape workforce" amid continued losses in its foundry division. The layoffs are concentrated in the US, Germany, and Israel, with severance packages covering 4–12 months of salary based on tenure.
