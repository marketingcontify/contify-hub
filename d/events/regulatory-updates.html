EU Regulation High Impact 🇪🇺 European Union

EU AI Act Full Enforcement Begins — High-Risk AI Systems Now Require Certification

The European Union's AI Act entered full enforcement phase, requiring all companies operating high-risk AI systems in EU markets to obtain conformity assessments and CE marking. General-purpose AI model providers with 10B+ parameter models must now register with the EU AI Office and submit technical documentation. Non-compliance penalties start at €15M or 3% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher.

FDA Grants Breakthrough Device Designation to Tempus AI's Cancer Genomic Sequencing Platform

The US Food and Drug Administration granted Breakthrough Device Designation to Tempus AI's xT CDx next-generation sequencing platform for solid tumor comprehensive genomic profiling. The designation accelerates the review pathway and positions Tempus as the first AI-native company to hold FDA clearance for clinical-grade genomic profiling across 648 cancer biomarkers.

Antitrust High Impact 🇺🇸 United States

DOJ Files Antitrust Suit Against Apple Over App Store Distribution Practices

The US Department of Justice filed an antitrust lawsuit against Apple alleging monopolistic practices in app distribution and in-app payment processing. The suit seeks structural remedies including mandatory third-party app store allowance on iOS and elimination of the 30% commission requirement. Apple faces potential court-ordered changes affecting its $26B annual App Store revenue.

SEC Ruling Medium Impact 🇺🇸 United States

SEC Finalizes Climate Disclosure Rules for Public Companies — Effective FY2027

The US Securities and Exchange Commission finalized its climate-related disclosure rules, requiring all public companies to disclose material climate risks, Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions, and climate-related financial impacts beginning with FY2027 annual reports. Large accelerated filers must also disclose Scope 3 emissions if material. The rules affect approximately 12,000 public companies.

Applies to: All US Public Companies

EU Fine High Impact 🇪🇺 European Union

Meta Fined €1.3B by EU Data Protection Commission for GDPR Data Transfer Violations

The Irish Data Protection Commission, acting on behalf of the EU DPC network, issued a record €1.3 billion fine against Meta Platforms for transferring EU user data to US servers in violation of GDPR Article 46. Meta has 6 months to comply with a data transfer suspension order. The company has appealed the fine in the EU Court of Justice.

Global Standard Medium Impact 🌍 Global

G7 Governments Agree on Voluntary AI Safety Framework for Foundation Model Developers

G7 member governments reached agreement on a voluntary AI safety framework requiring frontier AI developers to share pre-deployment safety evaluations with national AI safety institutes. The framework includes red-teaming requirements for models with capabilities above defined thresholds and establishes an international registry of certified frontier AI models.
