Google Leadership Team

Key executives, board members, and leadership changes at Alphabet and Google tracked by Contify.

25 tracked executives · 3 leadership changes in 2026
SP

Sundar Pichai

Chief Executive Officer, Alphabet & Google
Sundar Pichai leads both Alphabet Inc. and Google, overseeing the company's product strategy, AI initiatives, and global operations. He previously led the development of Chrome, Chrome OS, and Google Drive before becoming CEO in 2015.
CEO since 2015 · Joined Google in 2004
RP

Ruth Porat

President & Chief Investment Officer, Alphabet
Ruth Porat oversees Alphabet's investment strategy, capital allocation, and Other Bets portfolio. She previously served as CFO of Alphabet and Google from 2015 to 2023, and before that was CFO of Morgan Stanley.
President & CIO since 2023 · Joined Alphabet in 2015
TK

Thomas Kurian

Chief Executive Officer, Google Cloud
Thomas Kurian leads Google Cloud, driving its enterprise growth strategy and AI platform expansion. Under his leadership, Google Cloud has grown from $8B to over $40B in annual revenue. He previously spent 22 years at Oracle.
CEO, Google Cloud since 2019
DH

Demis Hassabis

Chief Executive Officer, Google DeepMind
Demis Hassabis leads Google DeepMind, the company's unified AI research lab responsible for Gemini and foundational AI advances. A Nobel Prize laureate in Chemistry (2024) for AlphaFold, he co-founded DeepMind in 2010.
At Google since 2014 (DeepMind acquisition)
PR

Prabhakar Raghavan

Senior Vice President, Knowledge & Information
Prabhakar Raghavan oversees Google Search, Ads, Commerce, Geo, and Payments products. A veteran computer scientist, he previously held leadership roles at Yahoo and IBM Research before joining Google in 2018.
SVP since 2020 · Joined Google in 2018
NM

Neal Mohan

Chief Executive Officer, YouTube
Neal Mohan leads YouTube, the world's largest video platform with over 2.5 billion monthly users. He was appointed CEO in 2023 after serving as YouTube's Chief Product Officer and has driven the platform's expansion into Shorts, podcasts, and live shopping.
CEO, YouTube since 2023 · Joined Google in 2008
HL

Hiroshi Lockheimer

Senior Vice President, Platforms & Ecosystems
Hiroshi Lockheimer oversees Android, Chrome, ChromeOS, Google Play, and the Pixel hardware ecosystem. He has been instrumental in growing Android to over 3 billion active devices worldwide.
SVP since 2016 · Joined Google in 2006
JF

Jen Fitzpatrick

Senior Vice President, Core Systems & Experiences
Jen Fitzpatrick leads Google's core infrastructure, including Maps, identity, and foundational platform services. She was Google's first female engineering vice president and one of the longest-tenured employees at the company.
SVP since 2019 · Joined Google in 2003
PS

Philipp Schindler

Senior Vice President & Chief Business Officer, Google
Philipp Schindler oversees Google's global business operations, including sales, partnerships, and go-to-market strategy across all products. He manages relationships with Google's largest advertisers and enterprise customers.
SVP & CBO since 2015 · Joined Google in 2005
KW

Kent Walker

President, Global Affairs & Chief Legal Officer
Kent Walker leads Alphabet's global policy, legal, and government affairs functions, including managing the company's antitrust defense, regulatory strategy, and AI governance initiatives across all jurisdictions.
President, Global Affairs since 2018 · Joined Google in 2006
