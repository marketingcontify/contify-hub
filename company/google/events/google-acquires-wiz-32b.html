Google Acquires Wiz for $32B in Largest-Ever Cloud Security Deal

Jun 15, 2026 Source: Reuters, Bloomberg, TechCrunch 5 min read

Alphabet has completed the acquisition of cloud security startup Wiz for $32 billion in an all-cash deal, marking the largest acquisition in Google's history and the biggest cybersecurity deal ever recorded. The transaction, which was first announced in March 2026, received regulatory clearance from the U.S. Department of Justice and European Commission in June.

Wiz, founded in 2020 by former Microsoft engineers, had previously rejected a $23 billion offer from Google in 2024, opting instead to pursue an IPO. However, the company ultimately accepted the revised $32 billion offer, representing a significant premium over its last private valuation of $12 billion.

Strategic Rationale

The acquisition gives Google Cloud a market-leading cloud security platform that protects workloads across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud environments. Wiz's agentless scanning technology and unified security graph will be integrated into Google Cloud's security operations suite, significantly strengthening its competitive position against Microsoft and CrowdStrike.

Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian stated that Wiz's multi-cloud security capabilities are critical as enterprises increasingly adopt hybrid and multi-cloud strategies. The deal is expected to accelerate Google Cloud's path to profitability and help close the gap with AWS and Azure in enterprise security.

Market Impact

The acquisition sent ripples through the cybersecurity industry, with shares of Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, and Zscaler declining on the news. Analysts at Goldman Sachs noted that the deal validates the growing importance of cloud-native security and could trigger further consolidation in the sector.

Wiz reported annual recurring revenue (ARR) of $500 million at the time of the deal, with over 40% of Fortune 100 companies as customers. The company will operate as a subsidiary of Google Cloud, with co-founder Assaf Rappaport continuing as CEO.
