Google Partners with Telstra for Australia's Digital Future

Jun 10, 2026 Source: Google Cloud Blog 4 min read

Google Cloud and Telstra, Australia's largest telecommunications company, have announced a landmark strategic partnership to accelerate Australia's digital transformation. The multi-year agreement will see Telstra leverage Google Cloud's AI infrastructure, including Gemini-powered analytics, to enhance network operations and customer experience.

Under the partnership, Telstra will migrate critical workloads to Google Cloud Platform, deploy Vertex AI for predictive network maintenance, and integrate Google's contact center AI to serve its 22 million customer accounts. The deal is valued at approximately $500 million over five years.

This partnership marks Google Cloud's largest enterprise deal in the Asia-Pacific region and strengthens its competitive position against AWS and Azure in Australia's rapidly growing cloud market, projected to reach $22 billion by 2028.

The collaboration also includes a joint innovation lab in Sydney focused on developing 5G edge computing solutions and exploring quantum-ready encryption for telecommunications infrastructure.
